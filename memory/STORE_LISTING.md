## Full description (Google Play / iOS, long form)

**Stop typing expenses. Start snapping them.**

BudgetSnavings is the fastest way to track your UPI spending in India. Just upload a screenshot of any GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or Amazon Pay payment — our on-device AI reads the amount, merchant and date, suggests a category, and adds it to your budget in seconds.

**Why people love BudgetSnavings**

📸  **Snap & log** — Upload a single payment screenshot or batch-import up to 10 at once. Edit anything before saving.

🧠  **Smart auto-categorisation** — Powered by Claude Sonnet 4.5 vision. Food, Groceries, Shopping, Transport, Bills, Entertainment, Health, Other — it knows.

📊  **Beautiful insights** — Monthly donut chart, multi-month trend lines, per-category sparklines and "vs last month" deltas.

💰  **Budgets that actually work** — Set a total monthly cap or per-category limits with your own alert threshold (50% to 100%). Smart suggestions based on your last 3 months — apply with one tap.

🔔  **Alerts that respect you** — In-app warnings the moment a budget reaches your threshold. No spam, no push fatigue.

🎯  **Savings goals** — Set a target, track contributions, and see how much is safe to spend today without missing your goal.

📤  **Own your data** — Export any month as CSV in two taps. No accounts. No tracking. No ads.

🇮🇳  **Built for India** — Native ₹ formatting, UPI receipt recognition, INR-first dashboards.

**Privacy first**
BudgetSnavings stores your data on your device + your private cloud workspace. We never sell or share your transactions.
